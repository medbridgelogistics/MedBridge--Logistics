About the company

Built for precision, resilience, and global healthcare delivery.

MedBridge Logistics is a European-based medical logistics specialist committed to moving the equipment hospitals depend on with the highest standards of care, compliance, and schedule reliability.

Our mission

Our mission is to ensure that critical medical infrastructure reaches hospitals, clinics, and surgical centers safely, on time, and ready for use. We combine regulatory expertise, secure handling protocols, and proactive communication to reduce risk for every shipment.

Whether we are transporting imaging equipment, surgical robotics, patient-care systems, or specialized laboratory technology, we focus on protecting both the equipment and the clinical timeline of our clients.

Our vision

Our vision is to be the trusted global logistics partner for high-value medical equipment moving between manufacturers, healthcare operators, and installation teams worldwide. We aim to set the benchmark for dependable, transparent, and intelligent logistics in the healthcare sector.

We believe every shipment is more than freight; it is a mission-critical link in patient care and hospital readiness.

Company history

Founded in 2009 and shaped by decades of healthcare logistics experience.

2009

MedBridge Logistics was founded as a specialist in medical freight coordination for Europe and the Middle East.

2014

We expanded our capabilities into temperature-controlled transport, customs coordination, and protected handling for delicate systems.

2018

Our teams grew into a multi-region operation supporting hospital groups, medical manufacturers, and infrastructure projects with tighter delivery windows.

2026

We continue to support high-value medical equipment projects across Europe, North America, and global corridors with full visibility and white-glove delivery standards.
